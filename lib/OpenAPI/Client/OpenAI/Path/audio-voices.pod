=head1 PATHS


=head2 C<POST /audio/voices>

Creates a custom voice.


Create a custom voice you can use for audio output (for example, in Text-to-Speech and the Realtime API). This requires an audio sample and a previously uploaded consent recording.

See the L<custom voices guide|https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/text-to-speech#custom-voices> for requirements and best practices. Custom voices are limited to eligible customers.
